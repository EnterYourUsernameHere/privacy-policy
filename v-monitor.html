V-Monitor Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 July 2026

V-Monitor is an Android application published by BigBaz. It allows users to connect to an existing Victron VRM account and view information from their Victron monitoring system. This policy explains how V-Monitor handles information.

Information handled by V-Monitor

Depending on the features a user chooses to use, V-Monitor may handle:

  • A Victron VRM email address, user ID, installation ID and authentication credentials entered by the user.
  • Approximate and precise location when location-based map or weather features are enabled.
  • Google Play purchase status used to unlock and restore purchased app features.

How information is used

This information is used only to authenticate with Victron VRM, retrieve and display Victron monitoring data, provide location-based map and weather features, and process or restore purchases made through Google Play.

V-Monitor does not use personal information for advertising or marketing and does not sell personal information.

Third-party services

To provide its features, V-Monitor may transmit relevant information to:

  • Victron Energy services for account authentication and monitoring data.
  • Weather services including wttr.in, Open-Meteo and MET Norway for location-based weather information.
  • Google Play Billing for purchases and entitlement restoration.

These third-party services process information according to their own privacy policies. The V-Monitor developer does not receive users' payment-card information from Google Play.

Storage and retention

Victron credentials and app preferences are stored locally on the user's device until cleared in V-Monitor or removed by uninstalling the app. Purchase entitlement may also be retained locally so purchased features remain available.

Third-party providers may retain information according to their own policies. Google Play retains purchase records according to Google's policies.

User choices and deletion

Location permission can be denied or withdrawn through Android settings. Users can clear saved Victron details from V-Monitor's settings and can remove locally stored app data through Android settings or by uninstalling V-Monitor.

V-Monitor does not create or manage Victron accounts. Requests to delete a Victron account or information held by Victron must be made through Victron VRM.

Security

User data transmitted by V-Monitor is protected in transit using HTTPS, TLS or SSL connections. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's privacy

V-Monitor is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when V-Monitor's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest revision.
